How to play

Three tools, one rule

Every level is a real thing frozen in a block of ice. Get all the ice off as fast as you can. Hit the thing itself and it cracks, and three cracks break it.

From level 1

Pickaxe

Tap to chip.

Each tap knocks out a whole chunk of ice. Tap quickly to chain combos for bonus Snowflakes, but don't tap the bare thing.

From level 3

Hairdryer

Hold to melt.

Hold it on the ice and the melt grows wider and faster. Watch the heat: it can overheat, and holding it too long on the bare thing scorches it.

From level 7

Scraper

Swipe to scrape.

Fast swipes clear wide strips of thin ice in one go. Thick Deep Freeze ice takes a little more work.

Features

Small game, lots to thaw

102 levels

Radios, plushies, teapots, toy cars and more. Every level is a different thing, and the ice gets thicker as you go.

New rules every few levels

Frost Creep ice that grows back, Fragile things that hate heat, and pickaxe-only or hairdryer-only challenges.

Race for 3 stars

Clear it fast for more stars. Some levels on the map only open once you've collected enough.

Upgrades and ice skins

Spend Snowflakes on a bigger, faster hairdryer and scraper, or turn the ice mint, strawberry, honey or midnight.

Daily Thing and Daily Spin

A new challenge every day with a streak bonus, plus a free spin on the prize wheel.

Build your collection

Every thing you save goes into My Things, your shelf of rescued stuff.

Is it free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. It shows an occasional ad between levels, and you can choose to watch an ad in the Shop for bonus Snowflakes.

It's on its way. Until it's live there, you can install it straight from this page.

Why does Android warn me about installing it?

Not yet. Right now the game is Android-only.

Do I need to be online?

No. You can play offline, and your progress is saved on your phone.
